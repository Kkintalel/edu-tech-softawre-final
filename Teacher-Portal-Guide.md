# Teacher Portal Guide

## Welcome
This guide explains how teachers use the school portal.

---

## 1. Log in to the Teacher Portal
1. Open the school website.
2. Click Teacher Login.
3. Enter your teacher email or username.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Login.

---

## 2. What teachers can do
- View assigned classes
- View class students
- Record marks
- Update attendance
- View assignments
- Manage timetable
- Communicate with students and parents
- Print report cards if needed

---

## 3. View students and results
1. Open the class dashboard.
2. Select the class or student.
3. Review marks and attendance.
4. Make updates where required.

---

## 4. Print student reports
1. Open the student record.
2. Go to the report or academic section.
3. Click Print Report Card.
4. Save as PDF or print.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting
- Check your password if login fails.
- Refresh the page if data does not load.
- Contact the administrator if you cannot access your class.

---

## Important notes
- Keep your login details secure.
- Update records accurately.
- Always logout after finishing work.
