# Student Portal Guide

## Welcome
This guide shows how students can use the school portal.

---

## 1. Log in to the Student Portal
1. Open the school portal website.
2. Click Student Login.
3. Enter your student email or username.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Login.

---

## 2. Student dashboard
After login, you will see your dashboard.

You can:
- view subjects,
- check grades,
- view attendance,
- view notices,
- see your timetable,
- print your report card.

---

## 3. View your report card
1. Open the dashboard.
2. Go to your academic or subjects page.
3. Click Print / Download Report Card.
4. Your report will open.

---

## 4. Print or save the report card
1. Click Print / Download Report Card.
2. Choose one of the following:
   - Save as PDF
   - Print to a printer
3. Click Print or Save.

### Printing tips
- Use Chrome or Edge.
- Use Portrait layout.
- Use A4 paper.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting

### I cannot log in
- Check your email and password.
- Make sure you are on the correct Student Login page.
- Contact the school office if needed.

### Report is not showing
- Contact your teacher or school office.
- Ensure your results have been entered.

---

## Important tips
- Do not share your login password.
- Use the official school portal only.
- Always logout after use.
