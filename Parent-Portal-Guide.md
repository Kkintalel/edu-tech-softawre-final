# Parent Portal Guide

## Welcome
This guide shows how parents and guardians can log in and use the school portal.

---

## 1. Log in to the Parent Portal
1. Open the school website or portal link.
2. Click Parent Login.
3. Enter your parent or guardian email.
4. Enter the student admission number.
5. Enter your parent password.
6. Click Login.

> Make sure you use the correct email and admission number for your child.

---

## 2. View your child’s dashboard
After login, you will see the parent dashboard.

You can:
- select your child,
- check fees,
- view payment history,
- pay school fees,
- view timetable,
- view academic progress report.

---

## 3. View student progress report
1. On the dashboard, select the child.
2. Click View Progress Report.
3. The student academic report will open.
4. You can review the marks, grades, remarks, and comments.

---

## 4. Print or save the report card
1. Open the progress report page.
2. Click Print.
3. A browser print window will open.
4. Choose one of the following:
   - Print to a printer
   - Save as PDF
5. Click Print or Save.

### Printing tips
- Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
- Allow pop-ups in the browser.
- Use A4 paper and Portrait mode for better printing.

---

## 5. Pay school fees
1. Open the parent dashboard.
2. Click Pay School Fees.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Choose the payment method.
5. Submit the payment.
6. You can also view payment history.

---

## 6. View timetable
1. From the dashboard, click View Timetable.
2. The timetable for your child will display.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting

### I cannot log in
- Check your email and password.
- Ensure you are using the correct student admission number.
- Contact the school office if you still cannot log in.

### The print page does not open
- Allow pop-ups in your browser.
- Try using Chrome or Edge.
- Refresh the page and click Print again.

### The report is not showing
- Confirm that the school has entered the student marks.
- Contact the school office for assistance.

---

## 8. Important notes
- Keep your login details private.
- Do not share your password with anyone.
- Always use the official school portal.

---

## Need help?
Contact the school office or administrator for support.
