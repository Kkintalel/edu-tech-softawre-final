# School Portal User Guide

This guide explains how to use the school management portal for all user types, including login, navigation, school operations, fees, reports, communication, security, and printing.

---

## 1. Overview of the Portal

The school portal is designed for different user groups, and each role has its own login page and access rights.

Users include:
- Parents / Guardians
- Students
- Teachers
- Admins
- Accountants
- HR staff

Each user should use the correct portal page for their role. The system keeps data secure and restricts access based on the user role.

---

## 2. How to Access the Portal

### General steps
1. Open the school portal website.
2. Select the correct login page for your role.
3. Enter your email or username.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Login.

### Recommended browsers
- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Firefox

### Browser tips
- Allow pop-ups for the portal.
- Keep cookies enabled.
- Use the latest browser version.
- Refresh the page if the dashboard does not load.

## 3. Parent / Guardian Portal

### How to log in
1. Open the school portal.
2. Click Parent Login.
3. Enter the parent or guardian email.
4. Enter the student admission number.
5. Enter the parent password.
6. Click Login.

### What parents can do
- View the parent dashboard
- Select a child
- Check school fees
- View payment history
- Pay school fees
- View timetable
- View student progress report
- Print or save a report card
- View school notices and updates

### Parent dashboard features
The parent dashboard usually includes:
- Child selection
- Student information
- Fees summary
- Payment status
- Quick actions such as payment, timetable, and report viewing

### How to view a child’s progress report
1. Log in to the parent portal.
2. Select the child from the dashboard.
3. Click View Progress Report.
4. Review subjects, marks, grades, teacher remarks, and comments.

### How to print or save a report card
1. Open the student progress report.
2. Click Print.
3. Choose one of the following:
   - Save as PDF
   - Print directly on paper
4. Click Print or Save.

### Parent printing tips
- Use Google Chrome or Edge.
- Allow pop-ups.
- Use A4 paper.
- Set layout to Portrait.

### Parent support checklist
- Keep your password private.
- Never share your admission number with unauthorized people.
- Contact the school office if a child is missing from the dashboard.

## 9. Fees and Payment Section

### For parents
Parents can:
- check total fees,
- see amount paid,
- see balance,
- pay fees,
- view payment history.

### Typical steps to pay fees
1. Log in as a parent.
2. Open the dashboard.
3. Select the child.
4. Click Pay School Fees.
5. Enter the amount.
6. Select the payment method.
7. Submit the payment.
8. Confirm the payment status.

### Notes
- Always confirm the correct student before paying.
- Save the receipt or transaction reference.
- Contact the school if payment is not reflected.

### School subscription terms
- Subscription plans may be Free, Basic, Professional, or Enterprise.
- Billing may be Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
- The subscription price, currency, student limit, teacher limit, class limit, and included features must be confirmed before payment.
- Payment status may be Paid, Pending, Failed, or Overdue.
- A subscription may be Active, Expired, Suspended, or Cancelled.
- Auto-renewal must be confirmed by the authorized school or Super Admin.
- Only an authorized Super Admin may create, update, suspend, or cancel a school subscription.
- Schools should keep payment receipts and transaction references.
- Subscription changes must be recorded and communicated to the school administrator.

### Supported subscription payment methods
- M-Pesa
- Bank transfer
- Credit card
- Cheque

### Payment and subscription support
For a payment or subscription problem, contact the Super Admin, school administrator, finance office, or technical support team. Provide the school name, subscription plan, payment method, transaction reference, payment date, and error message. Do not send passwords, PINs, or private payment credentials.

## 12. Security and Data Privacy

### Always do the following
- Use the correct login page.
- Keep your password private.
- Do not share login details.
- Log out after use.
- Only access data you are authorized to view.

### Important notice
If you see anything suspicious, contact the school administrator immediately.

### No reverse engineering
- Users must not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, copy, modify, or attempt to extract the portal source code.
- Users must not bypass authentication, role restrictions, payment controls, security settings, or audit logging.
- Users must not scan, probe, or attack the portal without written authorization.
- Users must not copy database structures, private APIs, credentials, or confidential school data.
- Only authorized developers may maintain or integrate with the system, and they must follow the school's written approval and security procedures.
- Reporting a security problem to the school administrator or technical support team is allowed and encouraged.

## 13. Role Conditions and Team Responsibilities

Every user must follow the conditions for their assigned role. Users must not use another person's account or access information outside their responsibility.

### Super Admin
- Must have the `SuperAdmin` role and an approved account.
- May manage all schools, school admins, subscriptions, academic years, logs, backups, and system reports.
- Must confirm the correct school or account before making changes.
- Must keep system-wide information confidential and review important actions in the audit logs.

### School Admin
- Must use an approved admin account for the assigned school.
- May manage that school's students, teachers, classes, subjects, fees, notices, reports, and settings.
- Must not manage another school's records or change system-wide Super Admin settings.
- Must verify important changes and maintain accurate school records.

### Teacher
- May access only assigned classes, subjects, and student information.
- May enter marks, update attendance, manage assignments, view timetables, and communicate through approved channels.
- Must check marks and attendance before saving.
- Must not change fees, user roles, passwords, or records outside assigned classes.

### Accountant
- May access authorized fee, payment, balance, and financial reports.
- Must confirm the correct student before recording or reviewing a payment.
- Must keep receipts and transaction references secure.
- Must not edit academic marks, attendance, or staff records unless separately authorized.

### HR Staff
- May access authorized employee, payroll, leave, and staff information.
- Must keep salary and employee records confidential.
- Must update staff information accurately and only when authorized.
- Must not access student academic or parent payment records unless permission is given.

### Parent or Guardian
- May access only children linked to the parent account.
- May view academic progress, fees, payment history, timetable, notices, and report cards.
- Must confirm the correct child before viewing reports or paying fees.
- Must not view another child's records or share admission numbers and passwords.

### Student
- May view only their own subjects, marks, attendance, timetable, notices, and report card.
- Must not edit marks, attendance, fees, or another student's information.
- Must report missing or incorrect information to the teacher or school office.
- Must log out after using a shared computer.

### Conditions for all teams
- Use the correct portal and personal account.
- Keep passwords and login information private.
- Access only information needed for assigned duties.
- Do not delete, edit, download, or share records without authorization.
- Check information before saving changes.
- Report suspicious activity, incorrect access, or data errors immediately.
- Log out after every session, especially on shared computers.

## 14. Troubleshooting Guide

### Problem: I cannot log in
Possible solutions:
- Check your email or username.
- Check your password.
- Use the correct portal for your role.
- Reset the password if required.
- Contact the school office if the problem continues.

### Problem: Page is not loading
Possible solutions:
- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Clear browser cache if necessary.
- Try another browser.

### Problem: Print dialog does not open
Possible solutions:
- Allow pop-ups in the browser.
- Use Chrome or Edge.
- Try again after refreshing the page.

### Problem: Report card is blank
Possible solutions:
- Wait until results are entered.
- Ask the teacher, class teacher, or admin.
- Confirm that the student has assessment data.

### Problem: Missing student or child in dashboard
Possible solutions:
- Check that the record is assigned to the correct parent account.
- Contact the school office for support.
