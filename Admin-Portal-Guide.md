# Admin Portal Guide

## Welcome
This guide shows how administrators use the school management portal.

---

## 1. Log in to the Admin Portal
1. Open the school portal website.
2. Click Admin Login.
3. Enter your admin email.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click Login.

---

## 2. What admins can do
- Manage students
- Manage teachers
- Manage classes and subjects
- View school reports
- Review fees and payments
- Manage notices and communication
- Update system settings

---

## 3. Manage students and teachers
1. Open the dashboard.
2. Select students or teachers.
3. Add, edit, view, or delete records as needed.

---

## 4. View reports
1. Go to the reports section.
2. Choose the report you need.
3. Review student or school data.
4. Print if required.

---

## 5. Troubleshooting
- Check login details if access fails.
- Refresh the page if data does not load.
- Contact technical support when the issue continues.

---

## Important notes
- Keep admin login credentials confidential.
- Only update records that you are authorized to change.
- Logout when you finish tasks.
